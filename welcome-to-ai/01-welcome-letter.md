# A Letter Before You Start

Hey —

So. Berkeley. Full scholarship. Library science *and* AI. I keep re-reading that sentence because I'm so proud of you I don't totally know what to do with myself.

I want to tell you something before the semester swallows you whole. The thing you're studying — how human beings find, keep, organize, and trust knowledge — is not a quiet little corner of the world. It's the whole game right now. Every argument about AI eventually becomes an argument about *information*: Who gets to decide what's true? How do we find the real thing in a sea of fakes? Who owns knowledge, and who gets locked out of it? Librarians have been asking those questions for a few thousand years. You're going to learn to ask them with new and very powerful tools.

A few things I wish someone had told me when I was starting something big:

**You belong there.** The scholarship isn't a fluke and it isn't charity. Somebody read your story and bet on you. When the impostor feeling shows up — and it will, probably around week three — remember that everyone feels it, and that feeling it means you're in a room worth being in.

**AI is a tool, not an oracle.** It will sound confident and be wrong. It will write you a beautiful paragraph with a fake citation in it. Your job — your whole training, really — is to be the person who can tell the difference. That makes you more valuable in the AI age, not less.

**Stay a beginner on purpose.** The fastest learners I know are the ones who keep asking "dumb" questions out loud. The field is moving so fast that everyone is a beginner at *something*. Being comfortable not knowing is a superpower.

**Don't let the machine think for you — let it think *with* you.** Use AI to go faster, see more options, understand hard things. Never use it to skip the part where *you* understand. The understanding is the thing you're actually there to get.

I made you this little package — guides, guardrails, things to try. It's not homework. It's a map you can ignore. Open it when you're curious or stuck, and close it when you've got it.

Go be brilliant. Call me when you find something cool. I want to hear all of it.

Love always,
your cousin 💛
