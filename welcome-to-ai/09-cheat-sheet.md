# The One-Page Cheat Sheet

*Print this. Pin it above your desk. It's the whole package in 60 seconds.*

---

### The golden rule
> **AI makes you faster. Never let it make you smaller.** Use it to think *with*, never to *skip* the thinking.

### The 5-part prompt — C.R.A.F.T.
- **Context** — who you are, what's going on
- **Role** — who the AI should act as
- **Ask** — the specific task
- **Format** — how you want the answer
- **Tone** — length + style

### Before you trust it
- **Verify every fact, name, date, and citation.** AI invents real-looking sources.
- **Ask:** does this make me understand *more*, or let me understand *less*? Aim for more.
- **If you can't explain it without the chatbot open, you're not done.**

### The trust thermometer
| Stakes | Verify how much? |
|--------|------------------|
| Brainstorming / explaining to yourself | Sanity-check |
| A draft you'll edit | Key facts |
| Anything you'll submit or cite | *Everything* — real sources |
| Health / legal / money / safety | Ask a qualified human |

### Never paste in
Passwords · other people's private data · confidential work · anything you'd put on a diary page, not a postcard.

### In class
Ask **before** each assignment: *"Is AI allowed here, and for what?"* When unsure, disclose how you used it.

### Your superpower (don't forget it)
You're training to be the person who can tell true from plausible, real source from fake, fair from biased. In the AI age, that's worth *more*, not less.
