## The one-paragraph version, if you read nothing else

AI is a tool that's astonishingly good at language and patterns and astonishingly confident when it's wrong. Treat it like a brilliant, fast, slightly unreliable intern: great for first drafts, brainstorming, and explaining hard things — but you are always the editor, the fact-checker, and the one whose name is on the work. Stay curious, stay skeptical, and never outsource your judgment. That's the whole game.
